Materials

Filament for every purpose.

We use quality filament so prints come out clean, strong and built to last.

PLADetailed, decorative and affordable prints.
PETGDurable and slightly flexible; good for functional, everyday parts.
ABSStrong and heat resistant for tough mechanical parts.
PCHigh strength and impact resistant for demanding parts.
ASAGreat for outdoor use, with excellent UV and weather resistance.
TPUFlexible and rubber-like; good for grips, bumpers, gaskets and parts that need to bend.

Not sure which to choose? We'll look at what the part needs and recommend the best balance of strength, finish and performance. Prices vary by filament type and quality, and other filaments are available on request.

Vapor smoothing: for the smoothest parts, ABS can be vapor smoothed. The surface melts slightly, which removes layer lines and leaves a glossy finish with little sign of being 3D printed.
