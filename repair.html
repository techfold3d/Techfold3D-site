Printer repair

Get your 3D printer printing reliably again.

What we repair

All FDM printers.

We repair all FDM (Fused Deposition Modeling) 3D printers, from calibration problems to full rebuilds, including troubleshooting, part replacement and upgrades.

We've worked on Ender, Prusa, Bambu, Snapmaker, Flashforge, MakerBot and Delta printers, and many others.

Pricing

Pricing is set after an initial assessment of the problem. If replacement parts are needed, their cost is added to the total.

Drop-off and pickup

Repairs are by drop-off in SW Airdrie, Alberta. We can also pick up your printer for you, which carries an extra charge that depends on your location. If you ship a printer or parts yourself, we can't be responsible for loss or damage in transit.
